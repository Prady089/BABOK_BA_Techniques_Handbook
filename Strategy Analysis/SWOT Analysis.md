# SWOT Analysis (BABOK® Technique)

## 1. What This Technique Is

SWOT Analysis is a strategic technique used by Business Analysts to **evaluate internal and external factors** that influence an organization or initiative.  
SWOT stands for **Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats**.

It provides a **structured snapshot of the current strategic position** and helps inform decisions about future direction.

---

## 2. Why This Technique Exists

Organizations make strategic decisions in environments shaped by:
- Internal capabilities and limitations
- External market forces and risks

Without structured analysis:
- Decisions rely on intuition
- Risks are underestimated
- Opportunities are missed

SWOT Analysis exists to help a BA:
- Create shared strategic understanding
- Balance internal realities with external forces
- Support informed strategic planning
- Identify focus areas for improvement or investment

BABOK includes SWOT Analysis because **strategy must be grounded in reality**.

## 4. When to Use SWOT Analysis

SWOT Analysis is most effective when:
- Defining or refining strategy
- Assessing feasibility of initiatives
- Evaluating market or organizational position
- Supporting business cases
- Initiating transformation programs

Common BA scenarios:
- Digital transformation initiatives
- Product or service expansion
- Process modernization
- Organizational restructuring

---

## 5. SWOT Components Explained

### Strengths (Internal, Positive)
Internal capabilities that provide advantage  
Examples:
- Strong domain expertise
- Mature processes
- Reliable systems

### Weaknesses (Internal, Negative)
Internal limitations or gaps  
Examples:
- Manual processes
- Skill shortages
- Legacy technology

### Opportunities (External, Positive)
External factors that can be leveraged  
Examples:
- New markets
- Regulatory changes
- Technology advancements

### Threats (External, Negative)
External risks that may impact success  
Examples:
- Competition
- Regulatory penalties
- Market volatility

---

## 6. How to Apply SWOT Analysis – Step by Step

### Step 1: Define the Scope
Clarify:
- Organization, product, or initiative being analyzed
- Time horizon

### Step 2: Gather Inputs
Use:
- Workshops
- Interviews
- Market research
- Performance data

### Step 3: Identify Strengths and Weaknesses
Focus on internal factors:
- Processes
- People
- Technology
- Culture

### Step 4: Identify Opportunities and Threats
Focus on external factors:
- Market trends
- Regulations
- Competitors
- Economic conditions

### Step 5: Analyze Relationships
Assess:
- How strengths can exploit opportunities
- How weaknesses expose threats

### Step 6: Derive Strategic Insights
Identify:
- Strategic priorities
- Risk mitigation actions
- Investment areas

## 9. Common Mistakes by Business Analysts

- Treating SWOT as a brainstorming-only exercise
- Mixing internal and external factors
- Creating vague or generic statements
- Failing to convert analysis into actions
- Not validating with stakeholders
