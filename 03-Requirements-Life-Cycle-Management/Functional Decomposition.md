# Functional Decomposition (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is
Functional Decomposition breaks down a complex business need into **smaller, manageable functions and sub-functions**.

It helps BAs:
- understand scope
- identify missing requirements
- organize requirements logically
- communicate complexity clearly

## Why This Technique Exists
Stakeholders often describe needs at a high level (“automate onboarding”).  
Decomposition turns that into structured components that can be:
- estimated
- prioritized
- designed
- tested

## When to Use
- Early analysis of large initiatives
- Defining scope boundaries
- Building a requirements structure (hierarchy)
- Preparing for estimation and delivery planning

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. Start with the top-level capability/function
2. Identify major sub-functions
3. Continue breaking down until items are actionable
4. Validate completeness with stakeholders
5. Use the decomposition as a navigation structure for requirements

## Output Example (Simple)
Top: Customer Onboarding
- Capture customer details
- Verify identity (KYC)
- Create account
- Assign products/services
- Notify customer
- Audit and reporting

## Common Mistakes
- Decomposing too deeply too soon
- Confusing functions with solutions
- Missing cross-cutting functions (security, audit)

## Related Techniques
- Scope Modelling
- Process Modelling

## Key Takeaway
Decomposition makes complex scope understandable and reduces missed requirements.
