# Change Control (BABOK® Technique)

## 1. What This Technique Is

Change Control is a technique used by Business Analysts to **manage, assess, approve, and track changes to requirements** throughout their life cycle.  
It ensures that changes are **controlled, evaluated, and aligned** with business objectives before implementation.

Change Control answers the question:  
**“What happens when someone wants to change a requirement?”**

---

## 2. Why This Technique Exists

Change is inevitable in any initiative. Without control, change leads to:
- Scope creep
- Budget and timeline overruns
- Confusion among stakeholders
- Rework and quality issues

Change Control exists to help a BA:
- Evaluate the impact of proposed changes
- Ensure informed decision-making
- Maintain alignment with business goals
- Protect solution integrity

BABOK emphasizes Change Control because **unmanaged change is a major source of project failure**.
