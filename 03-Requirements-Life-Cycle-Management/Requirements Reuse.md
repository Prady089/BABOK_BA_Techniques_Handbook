# Requirements Reuse (BABOK® Technique)

## 1. What This Technique Is

Requirements Reuse is a technique used by Business Analysts to **leverage existing requirements or requirement components** in new initiatives instead of creating them from scratch.  
It focuses on identifying **common, stable, and repeatable requirements** that can be reused across projects, products, or solutions.

Requirements reuse promotes **consistency, efficiency, and quality** in business analysis work.

---

## 2. Why This Technique Exists

Organizations often work on similar initiatives repeatedly, such as:
- Regulatory changes
- Product enhancements
- System integrations
- Reporting solutions

Without reuse:
- Analysts reinvent the wheel
- Inconsistencies increase
- Time and cost increase
- Quality varies across initiatives

Requirements Reuse exists to help a BA:
- Reduce effort and delivery time
- Improve consistency and standardization
- Leverage proven requirements
- Reduce risk by reusing validated content

BABOK includes this technique because **knowledge should be reused, not recreated**.

## 4. When to Use Requirements Reuse

Requirements Reuse is most effective when:
- Similar initiatives are repeated across the organization
- Regulatory or compliance requirements recur
- Products share common features
- Enterprise standards exist
- Time-to-market is critical

Common BA scenarios:
- Banking regulatory reporting
- Core system rollouts across regions
- Standard customer onboarding processes
- Shared enterprise platforms

---

## 5. What Can Be Reused

Reusable requirement assets may include:
- Business requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Regulatory requirements
- Business rules
- Acceptance criteria
- Data definitions
- Process fragments

Reuse does not mean copying blindly — **context always matters**.

---

## 6. How to Apply Requirements Reuse – Step by Step

### Step 1: Identify Reusable Assets
Locate requirements from:
- Past projects
- Templates
- Standards
- Repositories

### Step 2: Assess Suitability
Evaluate:
- Relevance to the new initiative
- Currency and accuracy
- Regulatory validity
- Assumptions and constraints

### Step 3: Adapt to Context
Tailor reused requirements to:
- New scope
- New stakeholders
- New systems or processes

### Step 4: Validate Reused Requirements
Confirm with stakeholders that:
- Requirements are still applicable
- No gaps or conflicts exist

### Step 5: Manage Versions
Ensure:
- Version control
- Clear ownership
- Change history

## 9. Common Mistakes by Business Analysts

- Reusing requirements without validation
- Ignoring context differences
- Failing to track reused items
- Treating reuse as copy-paste
- Using outdated requirements

---

## 10. Real-World Example (Banking / IT)

Scenario: Regulatory reporting across regions

A bank implements the same regulatory report in multiple countries.

Requirements reuse allows:
- Core regulatory rules to be reused
- Regional variations to be added
- Faster rollout with lower risk
- Consistent compliance interpretation
