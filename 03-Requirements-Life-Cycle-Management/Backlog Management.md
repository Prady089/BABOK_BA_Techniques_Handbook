# Backlog Management (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is
Backlog Management is the practice of **organizing, refining, prioritizing, and maintaining a set of work items** (requirements, features, user stories, defects, enhancements) so the team can deliver value incrementally.

A backlog is not just a list — it is a **living inventory of value**.

## Why This Technique Exists
Without active backlog management:
- priorities become unclear
- teams build low-value items
- scope creep grows silently
- delivery becomes chaotic

BABOK includes it because requirements must be **maintained and controlled**, not just documented.

## When to Use
- Agile product development (most common)
- Hybrid delivery with phased releases
- Continuous improvement environments
- Any initiative with frequent change

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. **Create backlog structure**
   - Epics → Features → Stories → Tasks
2. **Define each item clearly**
   - description, value, acceptance criteria
3. **Refine regularly**
   - split large items
   - clarify dependencies
   - remove duplicates
4. **Prioritize**
   - using MoSCoW, Value vs Effort, risk-based, WSJF (optional)
5. **Maintain readiness**
   - ensure top items are “ready” for delivery
6. **Track change**
   - re-prioritize based on feedback and constraints

## Outputs
- Prioritized backlog
- Refined stories with acceptance criteria
- Release and sprint candidates
- Dependency and risk visibility

## Common Mistakes
- Backlog becomes a dumping ground
- No clear definition of “Ready”
- Items lack acceptance criteria
- Priorities change without rationale
- Stakeholders bypass backlog governance

## Key Takeaway
Backlog management keeps delivery aligned to value and prevents uncontrolled scope.
