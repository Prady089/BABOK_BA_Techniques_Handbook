# 10.10 Collaborative Games

## 1. Purpose
Collaborative Games are structured activities or games used to facilitate collaboration and uncover hidden information, assumptions, and preferences from stakeholders in a fun and engaging way.

## 2. Description
These techniques move beyond traditional conversation-based elicitation. By creating a game-like, interactive environment, they encourage creative thinking, teamwork, and open communication. This can lead to a shared understanding and consensus that might be difficult to achieve through formal meetings.

## 3. Common Collaborative Games
- **Product Box:** Stakeholders design a box for the future product, forcing them to identify the most important features and benefits that would "sell" it.
- **Affinity Map:** Participants brainstorm ideas on sticky notes, which are then grouped (or mapped) into common themes by the team. This is great for organizing large amounts of information.
- **Buy a Feature:** Stakeholders are given a limited budget of "play money" to "buy" the features they want most from a list of potential features. This is a powerful prioritization technique.
- **Sailboat (or Speedboat):** Participants identify what helps the project move forward (the wind in the sails or the engine) and what holds it back (the anchors).

## 4. Usage
- To prioritize requirements or features.
- To brainstorm and define a product vision.
- To identify risks and project impediments.
- To build a shared understanding among a diverse group of stakeholders.
