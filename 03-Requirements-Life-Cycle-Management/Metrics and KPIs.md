# Metrics and KPIs (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is
Metrics and KPIs are measurable indicators used to **track performance, outcomes, and value**.

- **Metric**: a measure (e.g., processing time)
- **KPI**: a critical metric tied to objectives (e.g., reduce processing time by 30%)

## Why This Technique Exists
Solutions should improve outcomes, not just deliver features.  
Metrics and KPIs ensure:
- success is measurable
- value realization can be proven
- stakeholders stay aligned on outcomes

## When to Use
- Defining business objectives and business cases
- Establishing success criteria for solutions
- Measuring performance before/after changes
- Ongoing operational monitoring

## How to Apply (Step-by-Step)
1. Identify business objectives
2. Define measurable indicators aligned to objectives
3. Establish baselines (current performance)
4. Set targets (future performance)
5. Define measurement method and data sources
6. Assign ownership and review cadence

## Examples (Banking / IT)
- Reconciliation breaks per day (KPI)
- Average exception resolution time (KPI)
- STP rate (straight-through processing) (KPI)
- Data quality error rate (Metric)
- Uptime percentage (Metric)

## Common Mistakes
- KPIs not aligned to objectives
- Metrics are hard to measure or lack reliable data
- No baseline → no proof of improvement
- Too many KPIs (signal gets lost)

## Key Takeaway
Metrics convert business value from “claims” into measurable evidence.
