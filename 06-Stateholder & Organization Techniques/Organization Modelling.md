
# Organization Modelling (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Organization Modelling represents **roles, responsibilities, reporting structures, and interactions** within an organization.

---

## Why This Technique Exists

Many issues arise from unclear roles rather than poor systems.

BABOK includes Organization Modelling to:
- Clarify accountability
- Identify handoffs and gaps
- Support change impact analysis

---

## When to Use Organization Modelling

- Process redesign
- Operating model changes
- Role clarification
- Change management initiatives

---

## Real-World Example (Banking / IT)

Mapping Operations, Compliance, and IT roles during regulatory change to clarify approvals and ownership.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Models become outdated quickly
- Too much hierarchy detail
- Not linked to processes

---

## Key Takeaway

Organization Modelling reveals **people and responsibility impacts**, not just systems.
