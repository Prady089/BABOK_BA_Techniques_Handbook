# Benchmarking (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Benchmarking is a technique used to **compare an organization’s performance, processes, or practices** against:
- Industry standards
- Competitors
- Best-in-class organizations

---

## Why This Technique Exists

Organizations often ask:
- Are we performing well?
- What does “good” look like?
- How do others achieve better results?

BABOK includes benchmarking to help Business Analysts:
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Support business case justification
- Set realistic performance targets

---

## When to Use Benchmarking

Use benchmarking when:
- Planning improvement initiatives
- Evaluating competitiveness
- Supporting strategic decisions
- Justifying investment proposals

---

## Advantages

- Provides external perspective
- Encourages best practices
- Strengthens strategic decisions

---

## Limitations

- Comparable data may be limited
- Context differences can distort conclusions
- Benchmarks may become outdated

---

## Example (Banking / IT)

Comparing reconciliation turnaround times with peer banks to justify automation investment.

---

## BABOK Knowledge Areas Supported

- Strategy Analysis
- Business Analysis Planning & Monitoring

---

## Key Takeaway

Benchmarking provides **context and perspective**, but must be interpreted carefully within organizational constraints.
