# Business Capability Analysis (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Business Capability Analysis identifies **what an organization must be able to do** to achieve its objectives, independent of:
- Processes
- Organizational structure
- Technology

Capabilities represent **stable business abilities**.

---

## Why This Technique Exists

Projects often focus too narrowly on systems or processes.  
Capability analysis shifts focus to **outcomes and strategic alignment**.

BABOK includes this technique to help:
- Align initiatives with strategy
- Identify capability gaps
- Support portfolio and roadmap planning

---

## When to Use Business Capability Analysis

Use this technique when:
- Defining strategic initiatives
- Designing operating models
- Prioritizing investments
- Aligning multiple projects

---

## Advantages

- Strategy-driven
- Technology-agnostic
- Supports long-term planning

---

## Limitations

- Abstract for some stakeholders
- Requires strong facilitation
- Not immediately actionable without further analysis

---

## Example (Banking / IT)

Identifying capabilities such as “Regulatory Reporting”, “Risk Monitoring”, and “Customer Data Management” to guide transformation planning.

---

## BABOK Knowledge Areas Supported

- Strategy Analysis

---

## Key Takeaway

Business Capability Analysis ensures initiatives focus on **what the business needs to be able to do**, not just what systems to build.
