# Vendor Assessment (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Vendor Assessment evaluates **external suppliers or solution providers** to determine suitability based on business, technical, financial, and risk criteria.

---

## Why This Technique Exists

Vendor selection impacts:
- Cost
- Risk
- Compliance
- Long-term sustainability

BABOK includes Vendor Assessment to support **informed, defensible decisions**.

---

## When to Use Vendor Assessment

- Build vs buy decisions
- Tool or platform selection
- Outsourcing initiatives
- Regulated or high-risk solutions

---

## How Vendor Assessment Is Performed

1. Define evaluation criteria
2. Gather vendor information
3. Score vendors against criteria
4. Assess risks and dependencies
5. Recommend preferred option

---

## Real-World Example (Banking / IT)

Selecting a reconciliation tool by evaluating:
- Regulatory support
- Integration effort
- Cost of ownership
- Vendor stability

---

## Common Mistakes

- Focusing only on cost
- Ignoring long-term risk
- No documented evaluation rationale

---

## Key Takeaway

Vendor Assessment ensures **strategic, not reactive**, vendor decisions.
