# Acceptance Testing (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Acceptance Testing is the process of **validating that a solution meets business requirements and acceptance criteria** before it is formally approved for use.

It focuses on **business validation**, not technical correctness.

Acceptance testing answers one key question:
> “Does this solution satisfy the business need?”

---

## Why This Technique Exists

A solution can be technically correct and still fail the business.

BABOK includes Acceptance Testing to:
- Ensure requirements are implemented correctly
- Confirm the solution delivers expected value
- Provide formal approval before production release
- Reduce costly post-go-live issues

---

## When to Use Acceptance Testing

- Before go-live or release
- During User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- For regulatory or compliance-driven solutions
- When business sign-off is required

---

## How Acceptance Testing Is Performed

1. Define acceptance criteria (earlier in analysis)
2. Prepare acceptance test scenarios
3. Execute tests using real-world business cases
4. Capture results and defects
5. Validate fixes
6. Obtain formal business sign-off

---

## Real-World Example (Banking / IT)

**Scenario:** Regulatory reporting enhancement

Acceptance testing verifies:
- Mandatory regulatory fields are populated
- Reports reconcile with source systems
- Submission format meets regulator standards
- Audit logs are generated correctly

Only after validation does Compliance sign off.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Acceptance criteria defined too late
- Treating UAT as functional testing
- Business users not involved
- Skipping negative or exception scenarios

---

## Key Takeaway

Acceptance Testing protects the business by ensuring **requirements, not assumptions**, are delivered.
