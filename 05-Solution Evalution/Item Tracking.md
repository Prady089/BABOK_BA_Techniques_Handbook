# Item Tracking (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Item Tracking is used to **monitor the status, progress, and ownership** of requirements, issues, defects, risks, or action items throughout the lifecycle.

Tracked items may include:
- Requirements
- Change requests
- Defects
- Risks
- Decisions
- Action items

---

## Why This Technique Exists

Without tracking:
- Work gets lost
- Ownership becomes unclear
- Decisions are forgotten
- Accountability breaks down

BABOK includes Item Tracking to support **control and transparency**.

---

## When to Use Item Tracking

- Multi-stakeholder initiatives
- Long-running programs
- Regulated environments
- Any project with dependencies and handoffs

---

## How Item Tracking Is Used

1. Define item types to track
2. Assign unique identifiers
3. Capture status, owner, priority
4. Update regularly
5. Review during governance meetings

---

## Real-World Example (Banking / IT)

Tracking regulatory issues:
- Item ID
- Description
- Impacted regulation
- Owner (Compliance / IT)
- Status (Open, In Progress, Closed)

This ensures no regulatory gaps remain unresolved.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Tracking too many trivial items
- No ownership assigned
- Status not updated
- Tracking exists but not reviewed

---

## Key Takeaway

Item Tracking ensures **visibility, accountability, and follow-through**.
