# Reviews (BABOK® Technique)

## What This Technique Is

Reviews are **formal or informal evaluations** of work products to ensure quality, completeness, and alignment with expectations.

Reviewed items may include:
- Requirements
- Designs
- Test cases
- Deliverables
- Decisions

---

## Why This Technique Exists

Errors caught late are expensive.

BABOK includes Reviews to:
- Identify defects early
- Improve quality
- Ensure stakeholder alignment
- Reduce rework

---

## When to Use Reviews

- After requirements definition
- Before design approval
- Before testing or release
- During governance checkpoints

---

## How Reviews Are Conducted

1. Define review objectives
2. Share materials in advance
3. Conduct structured walkthrough
4. Capture feedback and actions
5. Resolve issues and re-review if needed

---

## Real-World Example (Banking / IT)

Reviewing regulatory requirements with Compliance before development to avoid rework.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Reviews treated as sign-off only
- Stakeholders not prepared
- Feedback not tracked or resolved

---

## Key Takeaway

Reviews improve quality by **catching issues early and collaboratively**.
